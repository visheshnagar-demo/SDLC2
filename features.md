# Project Features

## SCRUM-442 - Facultative Reinsurance Web Portal for Cyber & D&O Risks

### Feature Summary
A web portal for insurance carriers to submit facultative reinsurance requests for Cyber or D&O risks, featuring an underwriter review queue that scores submissions, flags missing information, and records quote or decline decisions.

### Key Features
- Carrier Submission Portal (Cyber & D&O risk submission forms)
- Underwriter Review Queue with Automated Risk Scoring
- Automated Missing Information Flagging & Document Checklist Rules
- Quote & Decline Decision Management with Real-Time Status Updates
