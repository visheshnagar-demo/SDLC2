# Project Features

## SCRUM-438 - Facultative Reinsurance Intake Portal and Underwriter Review Queue for Cyber & D&O Risks

### Feature Summary
Allows primary insurance carriers to submit Cyber and D&O facultative reinsurance requests, while providing underwriters with a queue to review risk scores, inspect missing data flags, and issue quotes or declines.

### Key Features
- Facultative Reinsurance Submission Intake Form for Cyber & D&O risks
- Underwriter Review Queue with status filtering and sorting
- Automated Risk Scoring Engine evaluating risk criteria and completeness
- Missing Information Flagging with prompt alerts to carrier representatives
- Quote/Decline Decision Recording with audit trail and notification workflows
